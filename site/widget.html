Today in TR's life

A rotating widget for trlibrary.com. It cycles through what Roosevelt was doing on today's date across his life, biggest days first, shown in year order. Each slide links to the full day. It works the same way as the visitor-quotes widget: one tag, four layouts, and it reads its background to choose light or dark text.

<div data-trpl-today data-layout="banner"></div>
<script src="https://daybyday.labs.trlibrary.com/widgets/today.js" async></script>

Banner

One year at a time, centered, changing every nine seconds. It reserves the height of the longest entry, so rotation never moves the rest of the page. Rotation pauses on hover or keyboard focus, when scrolled out of view, in a background tab, and for visitors who ask for reduced motion.

Card

The same rotation in a bordered panel, for a sidebar or column.

<div data-trpl-today data-layout="card"></div>

Wall

Three years at once, no rotation. Set data-count for more.

<div data-trpl-today data-layout="wall" data-count="3"></div>

Inline

A quiet rule-left version for the flow of a page.

<div data-trpl-today data-layout="inline"></div>

On any background

Nothing below is configured differently. On a photo or video section it switches to white text with a soft shadow. If a Drupal section hides its color from the widget, say what you want: data-text="white" or data-text="dark".

Options

AttributeWhat it does
data-layoutbanner (default), card, wall, inline
data-dateShow a fixed date instead of today, as MM-DD, e.g. 10-27 for his birthday
data-countHow many years to cycle through (default 6; wall 3)
data-minOnly include days at or above this significance, 1–5 (default 1)
data-intervalSeconds between slides; 0 turns rotation off (default 9)
data-text / data-themeForce white or dark text, or name the background as light or dark
data-alignleft or center
data-heightauto lets the block resize with each slide (default fixed)
data-ctaoff hides the “Plan your visit” link

Roosevelt's own words are left out of the rotation until each quotation is verified against the original letter, diary or speech.
