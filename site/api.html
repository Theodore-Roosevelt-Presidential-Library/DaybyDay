Use the data

Every day of Theodore Roosevelt's life is available as open JSON: where he was, who he was with, what he did, the letters he wrote, and his own words from his diary. Build a timeline, a map, a classroom exercise or a daily post. No key, no sign-up.

Widgets

New: Today in TR's life, a rotating banner, card, wall or inline block for trlibrary.com pages, built like the visitor-quotes widget.

Add one script tag to any page, then drop in a widget. Widgets pick up the page's fonts through the --dbd-display, --dbd-serif and --dbd-sans CSS variables, and accept theme="dark" and cta="off".

<script src="https://daybyday.labs.trlibrary.com/widgets/daybyday.js" defer></script>

<trpl-on-this-day count="3"></trpl-on-this-day>
<trpl-where-was-tr></trpl-where-was-tr>
<trpl-day date="1884-06-25"></trpl-day>

JSON API

Base URL: https://daybyday.labs.trlibrary.com/api/v1/. Every endpoint is a static file, cached at the edge and open to cross-site requests.

EndpointReturns
index.jsonDataset metadata, counts and the endpoint list
days/{YYYY-MM-DD}.jsonOne day: headline, summary, events, people, places, letters, TR quotes, tags, life periods, TRA page citations
on-this-day/{MM-DD}.jsonThat calendar date in every year of his life, most significant first
months/{YYYY-MM}.jsonCompact records for a month
years/{YYYY}.jsonCompact records for a year
people/index.jsonEveryone in the record, with group, description and day counts
people/{id}.jsonOne person, every day they appear, frequent companions and places
places/index.jsonEvery place, with coordinates and days present
places/{id}.jsonOne place and every day he was there
stays.jsonRuns of consecutive days in one place, for maps and journeys
periods.jsonLife stages, offices, journeys and campaigns, with dates
tags/index.json, tags/{tag}.jsonTopic tags and the dates carrying each
highlights.jsonThe days rated major or history-making
grid.json, search.jsonCompact per-day rows for visualizations and client-side search
 
fetch("https://daybyday.labs.trlibrary.com/api/v1/on-this-day/10-27.json")
  .then(r => r.json())
  .then(d => console.log(d.days[0].headline));

How it was made

The facts come from the Theodore Roosevelt Association's Chronological Record, a day-by-day calendar of Roosevelt's life compiled by Robert J. Moore Jr. in 1985 and held in the Theodore Roosevelt Collection at Harvard. Every day cites its pages in that record. The Library wrote each headline, summary and event description in its own words; the record's text is not reproduced.

Where he was comes from the record's place-names index, refined by the places named in each day. Days with no entry carry forward his last known location and are marked that way. Coordinates come from GeoNames and OpenStreetMap.

Roosevelt's own words (tr_quotes) are given as transcribed in the record and marked "verified": false. Check them against the original letter, diary or speech before quoting them in print. Each quote's source gives the record page it came from and, where possible, a link to the original: the diary page at the Library of Congress (1878–1884) or the letter at the Theodore Roosevelt Center.
