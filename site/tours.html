Tours and expeditions

Roosevelt was almost always going somewhere. Here are his big journeys, from a homesick ten-year-old's Grand Tour of Europe to the River of Doubt: whistle-stop campaigns, presidential speaking tours, hunting trips and expeditions. Open any one to see its route and read it day by day.

Each tour runs from the day he set out to the day he reached home (or the last day the Chronological Record has him traveling). Routes connect the places the record puts him, in order; days with no stated place are left off the line. Embed a tour on your site.
