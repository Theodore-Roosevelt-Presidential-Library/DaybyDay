Was he here?

Roosevelt crossed the country by train again and again, speaking from the back platform in towns big and small. Type a town or city to see whether he came, where he went nearby, and every day he spent in the state.

Try: DallasMedoraDenverBoiseNairobi

Places come from the Theodore Roosevelt Association's Chronological Record, which follows him closely but not every whistle-stop, so a town missing here is not proof he never passed through. Only days the record puts him in a place count (“probably here” marks days between entries, when he had not been seen to leave); places he only wrote about or acted on from afar, such as bird reservations he created, are left out. When a town isn't in the record, it is located with OpenStreetMap Nominatim (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Distances are straight-line miles.
